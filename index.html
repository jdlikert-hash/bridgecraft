Strategic Field Manual & Operations Guide

A tactical crisis communication, de-escalation, and multi-observer alignment system designed for Incident Management, Technical Support, and Engineering Leadership.

1 Executive Summary: The Incident Communication Problem

When high-severity incidents (Sev-1 / P1 outages) strike, communication breakdown is almost always the true bottleneck. Engineers retreat into technical weeds, executives demand instant binary answers, support agents absorb emotional heat from customers, and incident commanders clash over priorities.

BridgeCraft (formerly titled The Behavioral Profiler) is a zero-dependency, field-ready console designed to be kept open during critical operations. In under 45 seconds, an incident commander or manager can observe baseline cues, pinpoint an actor’s psychological distress pattern, and receive immediate, actionable tactical scripts (what to say vs. what to avoid on bridge calls and Slack) to de-escalate friction and restore operational alignment.

2 The Psychological Engine: Base vs. Phase

Built upon principles adapted from Dr. Taibi Kahler's Process Communication Model (PCM)—famously utilized by NASA for astronaut crew selection and crisis de-escalation:

Level 1: Base (Communication Channel)

The neurological baseline filter. Dictates how a person naturally perceives information: through Logic (Thinker), Values (Persister), Emotions (Harmonizer), Reactions (Rebel), Direct Action (Promoter), or Reflection (Imaginer).

→ Tactical Rule: Speak to their Base to be understood.

Level 2: Phase (Current Distress Driver)

The temporary motivational state under acute pressure. Dictates their distress tells: micromanaging (Thinker), moralizing (Persister), over-apologizing (Harmonizer), blaming (Rebel), steamrolling (Promoter), or shutting down (Imaginer).

→ Tactical Rule: Address their Phase need to de-escalate conflict.

3 DISC Leadership Alignment & The "Incident High-D" Reality

DISC is the universal language of corporate leadership (Dominance, Influence, Steadiness, Conscientiousness). Rather than forcing a bloated 20-question questionnaire during an outage, the Profiler automatically synthesizes DISC from the observed PCM channels:

Thinker → High C (Analytical)
Persister → High D/C (Evaluator)
Harmonizer → High S (Steadiness)
Rebel → High I (Influence)
Promoter → High D (Dominance)
Imaginer → High C/S (Reflective)

The Incident High-D Reality: In crisis situations, almost all executives and clients adopt an aggressive, situational High-D demand ("What's broken? When is it fixed?"). This tool tells you whether that person is a genuine Promoter or a panicked Thinker/Harmonizer needing specific reassurance.

4 Team Live-Fire Exercises & Situational Divergence

A core strength of the Dossier Library is supporting team training simulations:

  1. Multi-Observer Tracking: Multiple team members observe the same subject during a live drill and log cards independently.
  2. Card Ingestion: The lead clicks Import Cards to ingest all team `.md` cards in batch.
  3. Divergence Detection: The Dossier detects situational divergence (e.g. "Operates as Thinker during calm 1-on-1s, but shifts to Promoter distress during outages").
  4. Smart Deduplication: Use the Merge tool to consolidate misspellings (e.g. "Sarha" into "Sarah") while preserving all observer logs.
